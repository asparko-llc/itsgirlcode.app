In the app

Where the documents appear in the app. Screens from the current app (version 1.0.0, build 4), which is in review at the App Store and Google Play and not yet public. The links in the app open the published Terms and Privacy Policy on itsgirlcode.app, so they will show the final documents once these are approved.

1. At sign-up

Before using GirlCode, every new account sees One thing before we start, with three checkboxes. Agree and continue only goes through once all three are ticked:

  • I understand GirlCode is not an emergency service.
  • I understand GirlCode is a supplement, not a substitute.
  • I have read and agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, both linked.

2. Any time after sign-up

Both documents stay one tap away: Menu → Support → Legal, with Privacy policy and Terms of service.

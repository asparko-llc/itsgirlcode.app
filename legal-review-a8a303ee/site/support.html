Who sees my plan?

Only the people you add to it. You choose how they hear: a heads-up now, quietly, or only if you miss your check-in.

When does location sharing stop?

When you check in or end the plan. Location is only shared during a plan, and only if you turn it on.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password. We'll email you a code to set a new one.

How do I cancel GirlCode+?

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GirlCode Safety.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → GirlCode Safety.

You keep GirlCode+ until the end of the period you paid for. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open GirlCode+ in the app and tap Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, on your Profile. Steps and what gets deleted are on the delete account page.

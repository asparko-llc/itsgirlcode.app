Privacy Policy

At GirlCode, we care about your privacy and safety. That care extends to your data.

We collect only what GirlCode needs to work: your name, phone number, email, and the plans you create. On GirlCode+ (and during your first 5 plans), we also collect your location while a plan is active, if you turn it on, and the messages and photos you share on a plan.

Our commitment: We will always act with integrity when it comes to your data. We will never sell your data. We only share it with the companies that help us run GirlCode, and where the law requires it. Plans, and everything on them, are deleted 7 days after they end. We'll only keep what we need, and we'll be direct with you about how it all works.

What Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Your display name — what your circle sees you as
  • Your phone number — your unique identifier in the app (US format), verified once with a text code when you sign up
  • Your email address — for signing in and password reset codes
  • Your password — stored securely and scrambled, so no one, including us, can read it

Your Circle

  • The name and phone number of each person you add
  • When you add someone from your contacts, we only get the person you pick. We never upload your contact list.

Plan Data

All plans:

  • What you're doing, where (the words you type), who you're sharing with, and your check-in time
  • Check-in status — whether and when you checked in

On GirlCode+ plans (and your first 5 plans):

  • Your location — only if you turn it on for a plan and allow precise location; shared from the start of the plan until you check in
  • Messages — sent within that plan's conversation
  • Photos — attached within that plan's conversation

Subscription

  • Whether you have GirlCode+. Apple or Google handles payment, and we never see your card details.

Device Information

  • A notification token and an app installation ID, so we can send your check-in reminders and alerts from your circle

What We Don't Collect

  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools are in the app.
  • No audio. The microphone permission lets a fake call play through your earpiece like a real call. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • No location outside a plan. No always-on tracking, no map of your week.

What We Use Your Data For

We use your data only to run GirlCode. That's it.

  • Account information — to sign you in and let your circle find you
  • Plan data — lets you create plans and share them with your circle
  • Check-in status — lets your circle know you checked in, or that you missed your check-in
  • Location — lets the people on your plan see where you are until you check in
  • Messages and photos — let you talk with the people on your plan
  • Device information — delivers your reminders and alerts

We never:

  • Sell your data
  • Use it for advertising or marketing
  • Share it with anyone except the companies that help us run GirlCode, or where legally required

What we charge for: GirlCode+ includes location during your plan, plan messages and photos, saved plans, more than one circle, and custom fake calls. Your first 5 plans include everything. Plans, check-ins, reminders, missed check-in alerts, and preset fake calls are always free.

Why we charge for GirlCode+: There are people building this product. Servers, security, audits, keeping your data safe — that infrastructure costs money. GirlCode+ sustains it.

Who Can See What

The people you add to a plan can see:

  • The plan and your check-in status. You choose how they hear: a heads-up now, quietly, or only if you miss your check-in. With "Only if I miss it," they see the plan only if you miss your check-in.
  • Your location — only if it's on for that plan, and only until you check in
  • Messages and photos — only within that plan

You can see:

  • Your plans from the last 7 days and who you shared them with
  • The messages and photos within those plans

Service Providers

We use a few trusted service providers to run GirlCode, for things like hosting, phone verification, email, notifications, subscriptions, and maps. We share only what each one needs to provide its service to us. When a map loads, the map provider can see your device's IP address and the map area shown.

Location Privacy: The Details

Location sharing is a GirlCode+ feature, and it's part of your first 5 plans. Here's how it works and what's protected.

How it works:

  • You choose whether to turn on location sharing when you create a plan
  • The people on that plan see your location only while the plan is active
  • The moment you check in or end the plan, location sharing stops

What we protect:

  • Your location is never shared with anyone outside the people on that plan
  • Your location trail is kept for at most 48 hours during a plan, and deleted when the plan ends
  • One exception: if you don't check in and the plan ends automatically 24 hours after your check-in time, your last location stays with the plan for 7 days so the people on it can see it. Then it's deleted with the plan.
  • Location data is never sold

Messages & Photos

Messages and photos are a GirlCode+ feature, and they're part of your first 5 plans. They're tied to a specific plan and exist only within that plan's conversation.

  • When a plan ends, its conversation closes
  • Messages and photos are deleted with the plan, 7 days after it ends

Data Deletion

Plans, including their messages, photos, locations, and timestamps, are automatically deleted 7 days after they end. You can also delete any plan yourself, anytime.

You can delete your account at any time in the app, on your Profile. Your account details, circle, plans, and device information are deleted, and everything is permanently removed from our systems, including backups, within 30 days. Messages you sent on someone else's plan stay until that plan is deleted, no longer linked to your account. Records of phone verification attempts are kept for up to 30 days to prevent abuse, then deleted. Records of GirlCode+ purchases are kept by Apple, Google, and our subscription provider under their own policies, and we keep what the law requires for tax purposes.

You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing privacy@itsgirlcode.app. Steps are at itsgirlcode.app/delete-account.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections. Access is restricted so only you and the people you choose can see your plans. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your data and fix problems fast.

Age

GirlCode is for people 18 and older.

Where Your Data Lives

GirlCode is available in the United States, and your data is stored in the United States.

Your Rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data by emailing privacy@itsgirlcode.app. We'll respond within 30 days. Some state laws give you extra rights, and we'll honor these requests from any GirlCode user in the US.

GirlCode Is Not an Emergency Service

GirlCode helps your people know your plan. It doesn't contact emergency services. In an emergency, call 911.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.

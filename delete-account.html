Delete your account or data

You can delete your GirlCode Safety account at any time, right in the app.

In the app

  1. Open GirlCode and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Can't open the app?

Email privacy@itsgirlcode.app from the email address on your account, with the phone number you signed up with. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details: name, email, phone number and password
  • Your circle and the people in it
  • Your plans, with their location, messages and photos
  • Your device information for notifications

What's kept, and for how long

  • Copies in backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Messages you sent on someone else's plan stay until that plan is deleted (plans are deleted 7 days after they end), no longer linked to your account.
  • Records of phone verification attempts are kept for up to 30 days to prevent abuse, then deleted.
  • Records of GirlCode+ purchases are kept by Apple, Google and our subscription provider under their own policies, and we keep what the law requires for tax purposes.

Delete some of your data, keep your account

You can delete any of your plans in the app. Deleting a plan removes it along with its location, messages and photos, right away. Your account and circle stay as they are.

Plans you don't delete are removed automatically 7 days after they end.

To ask us to delete other data without deleting your account, email privacy@itsgirlcode.app from the email address on your account and tell us what you'd like removed.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel GirlCode+. Cancel it in your Apple or Google Play subscription settings. Steps are on the support page.
